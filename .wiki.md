# Project Summary
The project focuses on developing and implementing marketing strategies for Sodium Thiosulfate through Google Ads. It aims to enhance the visibility and sales of Sodium Thiosulfate products by leveraging targeted advertising techniques, optimizing campaigns, and analyzing market trends.

# Project Module Description
The primary module of this project is the Google Ads Strategy for Sodium Thiosulfate. This module encompasses the following functionalities:
- **Campaign Optimization**: Adjusting market strategies, forecasts, and keyword targeting.
- **Landing Page Development**: Creating optimized landing pages for existing products or new offerings.
- **Competitor Analysis**: Conducting in-depth analysis of competitors in the market space.
- **Other Product Strategies**: Developing advertising strategies for related industrial products.
